---
title: Deleting an item should ask first
state: New
category: Improvement
---

"Delete selected" removes the row immediately. One misplaced click and the
line is gone.

**Acceptance criteria**

- Clicking "Delete selected" with a row selected shows a confirmation dialog
  naming the item.
- "Yes" removes the row and updates the totals; "No" leaves everything as is.
- With no row selected the button does nothing, as today.
